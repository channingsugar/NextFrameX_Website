Keyboard shortcuts

Look up a shortcut by stage of the work. These are shown for macOS. On other systems the main modifier is usually Ctrl.

English guide · Updated 2026-10-17 · Interface reference 2.54.0

Check focus first

While typing in a text field, letter keys type characters. A dialog, curve edit, crop tool, or mask selection can change what a shortcut does. Click back into the workspace you mean. A press-and-hold is a different command from a short press.

⌘ is Command. ⌥ is Option. Groups of keys are separated with “／”.

Sampling and masks

Hold Q to choose Dmax. A short press of Q samples Dmin. In the mask shortcuts, · / the backtick sits at the top left of the keyboard. Other layouts may show a different character. You can use the panel buttons instead.

The filmstrip and the mask list both use ⌘ A, ⌘ D, and similar combinations. Before a batch command, check what is selected so a photo command is not applied to a mask.
