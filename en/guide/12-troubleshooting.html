Troubleshooting

Find the symptom, then the check that goes with it.

English guide · Updated 2026-10-17 · Interface reference 2.54.0

Sprocket holes missed, or the picture filled white

Press G to check the switch, and hold G to adjust the threshold. Raise it a little when holes are missed. Lower it when the picture or the edge codes are filled by mistake. Zoom in before you confirm.

Fog, low contrast, or an obvious cast

Press V and check the exposure range and Dmin. Shrink the range if it includes interfering color. If the picture has no black point, try including a little film base. You can place Dmin again on the narrow black edge or in the gap between frames. Clear it if the result gets worse. Then follow Color.

Cloud color correction fails

Check the network and the service connection. A system-wide VPN can block NextFrame’s color service. If it cannot connect, turn cloud correction off for now and keep using local inversion and hand adjustments. Turn it back on when the network returns.

A shortcut does nothing

Leave the text field or dialog and click the Develop workspace. Confirm whether a photo or a mask is selected, and tell a short press from a press-and-hold. See Keyboard shortcuts.

A feature is missing, or a button is unavailable

Check the installed version and the current mode. Some features need a camera, a device, or an account permission. HDR output, camera live view, and automatic dust removal follow the options the interface actually offers.
