01 / Digital darkroom

A film darkroom.
From inversion to the final file.

A density-domain inversion engine, a full set of color tools, and an automated path through capture, scanning, development, and output. Darkroom control, in one workspace.

01

Density inversion

Start from the film’s density, sample the base, and use DCMY to rebuild the negative’s color and tone.

 02

Automatic RAW

Import and go. Detect the frame, straighten, invert, and balance color, and let the roll’s repetitive work run itself.

 03

FFF auto crop

Find frames in a strip scan and make a crop for each one. A strip comes in. Frames come out, each one editable.

 04

Many formats

Bring camera RAW, DNG, TIFF, and FFF scans into the same darkroom, whatever the camera or scanner.

 05

Sprockets and borders

Keep edge codes, fill sprocket holes white, and use the LIVE border template. The film edge can stay part of the picture.

 06

HDR output

From the develop preview to Ultra HDR, so bright sources and fine tone hold up on screens that support it.

 07

Full color control

DCMY, split white balance, curves, a mixer, color grading, LUTs, and local masks. From a faithful print to a personal look.

 08

Controls on the image

Hold a key for the dial, drag a waveform, and keep moving with shortcuts. Strip crops keep a whole roll in one rhythm.

 09

Tethered capture

Connect a supported camera for live view, remote release, and ingest. Or watch a folder and continue with the copy setup you already use.

 10

On-device dust removal

Detect dust on this computer, edit the detected area, then repair it. Automatic detection and a manual brush, without inventing detail.

 11

LIVE motion

Turn a still frame into a short motion piece. macOS can save a Live Photo. Windows can export a Live video.

 12

Works with agents

Connect a supported agent through local MCP. Ask it to invert, crop, and grade, and take the file back for hand editing whenever you want.
