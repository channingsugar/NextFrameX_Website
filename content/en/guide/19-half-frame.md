# Half-frame mode: process two pictures in one scan

When you copy or scan two half frames together, you can keep them side by side. NextFrame finds an exposure area for each picture and processes their colors separately.

## Run Auto on half-frame pairs

1. In the directory, select multiple scans from the same roll. Check that each scan shows two complete pictures side by side.
2. Hold **A** and choose **Auto**. In the batch dialog, select **These frames are from the same roll**, set **Frame format** to **Half-frame**, and continue.
3. Open a processed scan and press **V** to see the exposure areas. Each box should sit inside its own picture, clear of the central divider, sprocket holes and adjacent frames.

![Separate exposure boxes around a waterfall on the left and a woodland path on the right, both inside the film edges](../assets/media/half-frame-exposure.webp)

In this scan, the bright waterfall contributes to the exposure calculation on the left. The shaded path has its own sampling area. To fine-tune the left picture, use the global controls. To adjust the right picture, select the mask covering the right half.

## Keep the pair or export individual pictures

Export the current scan to keep the two pictures together. For two separate files, ask Codex to create a left copy and a right copy from the processed pair, then export both. Each copy carries over the adjustments for its own frame. For scans containing a whole strip, see [Split a film strip into individual frames](04-strip.html).
