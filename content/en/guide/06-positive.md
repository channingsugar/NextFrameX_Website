# Slides and black-and-white negatives

Slides use the original-image path. Black-and-white negatives are inverted, then adjusted for gray and tone.

## Slides

1. Import as the original image and confirm that negative inversion is off. Press `C` to toggle inversion.
2. `P` automatic color correction is on by default, with levels stretch at 1. If the cloud service is unavailable, adjust by hand first.
3. Press `F` to check the crop and `V` to check the range.
4. Adjust exposure, white balance, and curves, and compare the picture with automatic color correction on and off.
5. Press `⌘ S` to export.

If import preferences turn inversion on directly, check a slide after import and turn inversion off.

## Black-and-white negatives

Import and invert them as negatives. After the crop and analysis range look right, press `B` to turn on grayscale. Use the D waveform, tone controls, and curves for the light-to-dark relationship. For a tinted black-and-white look, turn grayscale off and use color grading.

Zoom in on highlights and shadows, then add sharpening, glow, and grain if you want them.
