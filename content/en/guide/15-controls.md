# Dials and waveforms

The dial picks a mode or adjusts a setting quickly. Waveforms control inversion directly. The common moves can happen in the preview.

## Hold a key to open the dial

Focus the Develop workspace, hold the key, move the pointer to an item on the dial, and finish the choice the way the interface indicates. A short press of the same key runs a different shortcut.

| Hold | Function |
| --- | --- |
| A | Exposure-range presets, automatic processing |
| D | Automatic crop mode, straighten, no crop |
| S | Vivid, Natural, or Original tone |
| Q | Choose Dmin or Dmax sampling |
| T | Temperature and tint |
| G | Sprocket-fill threshold |
| Z | LUT on/off and strength |
| 2, 3 | Custom dials |

In Processing Preferences, set the controls for Custom (2) and Custom (3). After you choose the adjustments you use often, hold the matching number key while editing.

## Drag a waveform

Open the inversion panel and drag the left or right end of the D, C, M, or Y waveform. The left end controls the baseline. The right end controls gain. D adjusts the whole picture. C, M, and Y are the red, green, and blue channels.

Move a little, look at the picture, then continue. If shadows and highlights have different casts, adjust the matching end. Do not change the color only to make the waveforms overlap. More detail is in [Color](08-color.html).

## Move through the photos

Use the left and right arrow keys to change photos. `N` compares, `V` shows marks, `F` crops, `⌘ C` / `⌘ V` copy and paste settings, and `⌘ S` opens export.

After a strip scan, press `F` to crop, choose Strip, and set every box with frame count, group count, spacing, and offset. See [Strip scans](04-strip.html).

The full key list is in [Keyboard shortcuts](11-shortcuts.html).
